What I'm Building

I work on WordSen every day. Here's what's happening.

Beta launches late May 2026

I aimed for April. I missed it. The app needed more polish before strangers used it, and I'd rather hand testers something solid than rush. The first wave is small (5-10 people) so I can act on feedback fast.

If you're learning Japanese and have an iPhone, get on the list. There will be more waves after the first one.

What I've shipped

May 2026

Charms

Built a charm system. Before each study session you pick one of eight charms (if you have any) and it changes how you earn rewards for that session. Some give bonus koins. Some buff specific cards. One gives a chunky payout if you finish what you started. They drop after sessions, so you build up a collection over time. The end-of-session screen got a full visual rewrite to match.

The trophy case

The Word List used to look like a generic grid. Now mastered cards bear a kanji brushwork stamp tied to the level they came from. If you added an image to a card, it earns a red seal stamp on top of that. Scroll through 200 mastered cards months from now and you can SEE the work you put in. It's the wallpaper.

Offline mode that actually works

Found a nasty bug where rating a card with no internet would silently hang the app. Fixed it properly. The Study button now blocks when you're offline (with a clear message), and audio for cards you've already heard plays from a local cache. So if you're on the subway, your previously studied cards still work.

Better animations everywhere

Reworked how rewards feel. Energy and koins now fly across the screen as little chips that land in their pills with a satisfying pulse. The progress bar fills with a snappy spring. Layered haptics for the rating buttons. The whole study loop feels MUCH more alive.

WordList loads instantly

It used to take 1-2 seconds to open the Word List. That was unacceptable. Tracked it down to a calculation that was running about 2 million times per render. Cached the result. Now it's instant.

Hide and delete

You can now hide cards from your study list (for built-in words you already know cold) or delete cards you created. Hidden cards keep their progress in case you want to bring them back later.

April 2026

The "MAX" energy fix

If your energy was already maxed out, the app would still play the "+5 energy" animation when you finished a card. Felt dishonest. Now the pill just shows "MAX" and the animation only fires when energy actually goes up. Small change, but the kind of thing that adds up.

Momentum celebration you can actually see

The daily momentum hit had a tiny ring bump that nobody noticed. Now it's a full Balatro-style stamp with confetti and layered haptics. When you cross the line for the day, you KNOW.

Typing got fixed

Bug reports, card reports, and Oracle notes all had the same problem: the cursor would disappear under the keyboard while you were typing. Rebuilt those screens so the keyboard plays nice. Typing feels responsive now.

New study header

The top of the study screen used to be cluttered with progress info. Replaced it with a clean rainbow-arc progress ring and a display options menu (toggle furigana, romaji, English, image blur). Way less noise, easier to focus.

Smarter bug reports

Submitting a bug report used to require two taps (one for the report, one for diagnostics). Now it auto-attaches the diagnostic data when you send the report. One tap, both signals. Way better for me when I'm trying to figure out what went wrong.

Cloud Functions live in production

All six server-side functions are now running in production. Card creation, Oracle questions, audio generation, transcription, and the unlock pipeline all run on the cloud now. This makes everything faster AND more secure.

March 2026

Card creation got FAST

Moved the AI stuff to the cloud and card creation went from 15 seconds to about 4. That's a HUGE difference when you're trying to capture a word on the fly.

Oracle learned to listen

Redesigned the Oracle AI assistant. You can talk to it now instead of typing, and it has a personal notebook for saving what you learn. Two tabs: Ask and Notes. Clean and simple.

Browse your whole vocabulary

Built a word list so you can actually see everything you're studying. Filter by status (up for review, in progress, mastered) and watch your progress grow through all 1,000 words.

Security lockdown

Did a full security audit. Database protection rules, encrypted storage, cleaned up logging. Not glamorous, but REALLY important before letting other people use the app.

February 2026

Dango, mochi, and taiyaki

Your Kitsune can eat three different treats now, each one giving a one-hour study buff. You can also pet your fox. Little hearts float up. It's adorable.

Hide and seek (but for koins)

Added a mini-game where your fox sniffs out buried koins with a little celebration dance when it finds them. Spend the koins on furniture for the room.

January 2026

The fox got smarter

Rebuilt how the Kitsune moves around its room. It navigates furniture without getting stuck and looks way more natural. Also added close-up cameras for when you feed or pet it.

December 2025

Furniture shopping!

Added a store, an inventory system, and drag-and-drop room placement. Buy items with koins you earn from studying, then make your fox's room look however you want.

November 2025

The Kitsune comes alive

Imported the 3D fox model with full animations. Walking, running, idle, jumping. You can tap and swipe the camera around. This was the moment the app started feeling REAL.

October 2025

Flashcards that actually work

Launched the core study experience. Spaced repetition, male and female audio, custom images, furigana on everything. This is the foundation the whole app is built on.

September 2025

Say it and study it

Added voice card creation. Record a word, WordSen figures out what you said, generates audio and an example sentence, adds it to your deck. This was the feature that made me decide to build the whole app.

August 2025

We're live

Launched the WordSen website, started writing the dev blog, and opened beta signups.
