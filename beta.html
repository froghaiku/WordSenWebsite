Help me build WordSen

I need a small group of people learning Japanese to bang on the app and tell me what's broken. That's it.

Here's the deal

Who you are

You know your hiragana and katakana. Maybe a handful of words. You've tried other apps and hit a wall, or you're afraid you will. You're the kind of person who'll send me a screenshot of something weird instead of silently uninstalling.

What I need from you

Use the app. Study some flashcards. Take care of your Kitsune. Tell me what works, what doesn't, and what's confusing. A two-sentence message about a weird interaction is more valuable than a five-star review. Trust me.

What you get

Free access to the full app during the beta. Direct line to me when something breaks. A say in what gets built next. Plus 1,000 koins on launch day if you're one of the first 100 signups.
